A React rebuild of two earlier prototypes (an unfinished storefront and an Express/SQLite inventory app) that turned out to be the same business split in two. The plan: a store + blog for customers, and an admin panel with content moderation plus inventory/recipe management — each candle's cost and price are computed automatically from its materials' prices. Right now only the planning (data model, screens, phases) exists, split across three new repos; development hasn't started yet.
